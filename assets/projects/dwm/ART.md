## Universe: timeline (16:9, shown small)

- · `universe/timeline/ikon-wars.webp` Before time: God the Father, Gyvenimas and Mirtis decide to end the wars of the Ikon. Vita and Éres are born.
- · `universe/timeline/vita-eres.webp` Before time: Vita bears Herggott. Éres kills her and Reficul is born from her womb. Éres is locked in the deepest dungeon of hell.
- · `universe/timeline/gyvenimas-scroll.webp` Before time: Gyvenimas leaves Heaven and throws her scroll of life to the earth. Mirtis falls after it and is trapped among men.
- · `universe/timeline/creation.webp` Year 0: God the Father creates the world. The Ikon receive the Prism, reason and their animal form. Humanity is born.
- · `universe/timeline/scroll-found.webp` 629: Zeus finds the greatest treasure in history: the Scroll of Resurrection, the power of Gyvenimas.
- · `universe/timeline/demons-named.webp` 800: The black gods take the name of demons, and Reficul becomes their leader.
- · `universe/timeline/ricardo-born.webp` 892: Ricardo Monteoro is born.
- · `universe/timeline/war.webp` 1028: The War between Light and Darkness. The Celestial Union and the Army of Light are founded. The demons are banished.
- · `universe/timeline/first-humans.webp` 1112: The first humans join the Celestial Union.
- · `universe/timeline/dionysus-union.webp` 1208: Dionysus, god of wine and pleasure, joins the Celestial Union.
- · `universe/timeline/cristian-i-born.webp` 1460: Cristian D’ San Fernald I is born in Rome.
- · `universe/timeline/cristian-born.webp` 1492: Cristian D’ San Fernald II is born in Tuscany.
- · `universe/timeline/elizabeth-born.webp` 1495: Elizabeth Gloucester is born in London. Her mother Margaret dies the next day.
- · `universe/timeline/train.webp` 1507: Cristian’s parents die on a train in Scotland.
