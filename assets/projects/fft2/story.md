# Final Fantasy Tactics II: The Moon and the Radiant Sword

Only what the game tells openly; the secrets stay in the game.

## Prologue I: The King Who Fell

*History sings loud... of the lowborn hero who unified a shattered realm. They called his ascension the miracle of Ivalice...*

Rain falls on the royal palace of Lesalia. Inside, a man staggers through cold stone halls, clutching a wound in his side. He has no crown. He calls for help, and nobody answers: the guard posts are empty, the banners abandoned.

*Barely a year into his stolen reign... the King was no more. He left behind no heirs, no bloodline, and no crown to steady the realm. And so... heaven turned its back.*

King Delita Heiral rests today in the cemetery of Lesalia, under a stone with his name. The history books say he ruled in peace.

## Prologue II: Those Who Survive

*But history is written by those who survive to tell it...*

Far from the palace, two exiles flee into the mountain mist: a sworn blade of the Church and a keeper of forgotten machines. They leave behind their noble names, their titles and the songs that called them heroes, and they vanish.

## Prologue III: Twenty Years of No Man’s Land

*When the ancient demons fell... and the skies cleared of the Lucavi’s terror... the true nightmare of the realm began.*

Ivalice was left without a king. Famine spread across the plains, plague swept through the towns, bandit clans ruled the roads, and the soil itself was still sick with the corruption of the broken Zodiac Stones. The great houses lost everything and had to dig themselves out of the ashes, clearing cursed land and fighting off starving mobs just to stay alive.

*For twenty long years, Ivalice became a forgotten no man’s land. A country left to rot, to bleed, and to slowly rebuild itself from absolute zero.*

## Prologue IV: The Hidden Sanctuary

*Far from the eyes of the world... in a quiet, hidden sanctuary... a new life began to breathe.*

In a rustic home hidden in the volcanic valleys outside Goug, a former Holy Knight and a machinist hold their newborn daughter. They named her Aveline.

*But... peace is a fragile thing. For beyond the borders... dark designs were already taking shape in the shadows.*

## Chapter 1: The Compass Awakens

*The Path of Light.* Twenty years have passed. In the hidden homestead, Aveline adjusts the straps of her training brigandine after another dawn of sword drills. Her mother, Agrias Oaks, watches from a rocking chair, her left leg stiff from an old wound. To Ivalice, Agrias is a heretic who vanished with the rogue Ramza Beoulve. To Aveline, she is simply her mother.

Aveline is thinking of her father. Five years ago, an earthquake trapped the miners beneath Goug, and Mustadio Bunansa went down into the collapsing tunnels to save them. He did, and a second cave-in took his life. From those tunnels came his last gift: a mechanical compass of lost technology whose gears never need winding.

For years Aveline has seen the same vision while awake: Aethelgard, a sword of pure light buried in a distant ruin, calling her name through the Mist. Now, as the houses of Ivalice announce the Leadership Games, the compass clicks. Its gears spin wildly, the needle breaks from the north and points northeast, to the heart of Ivalice, and the glass blazes with the same light from her dreams.

Agrias recognizes that light at once. Twenty years of hiding are over. She unlocks the iron-bound trunk that has stayed closed since the war and places her old Holy Knight armor in her daughter’s hands. The world is moving again, she tells her: follow your father’s compass and do what my generation could not.

## Chapter 2: The Heir Ceremony

*The Path of the Moon.* Across the frontier mountains, the capital of Ordallia roars with trumpets. King Marcel Bourdeaux has called the Grand Heir Ceremony to name his eldest son, Valerand, sole heir to the throne.

Prince Lucius walks the palace halls under his mother’s crest, a crescent moon swallowing a star, a reminder that he is the second son, the spare, always a step behind his brother. In the royal armory, his eyes go, as always, to Dawnweaver, the ancestral sword of the house, revered across the empire as a gift from the gods. It belongs to his father, and in a few days it will pass to Valerand. Lucius has never been allowed to touch it.

Valerand finds him there and mocks him: admiring what will never be his. Before the argument can grow, their sister Genevieve steps between them. Envoys from Romanda have arrived, she warns, and they are watching the family for any sign of weakness.

Lucius straightens his cloak. The great doors of the throne room open onto thousands of cheering nobles, and he takes his place a step behind his brother, unaware of the light traveling north from Goug, and of what sleeps in the steel of Dawnweaver.

## Chapter 3: Roads to Lesalia

Agrias sends her daughter first to Romanda, to the old workshop of Mustadio’s father, Besrudio, certain she will find something there. In the world of commoners Aveline is Aveline Bunansa; before the nobility she is Lady Aveline Oaks, a name that opens doors and draws the eyes of the Church. As the visions continue, Agrias sends her on to her ancestral home in Lesalia, to claim the inheritance she will need for the journey ahead.

In Ordallia, King Marcel gives his second son a mission worthy of a diplomat. Lucius will ride to Lesalia as the royal envoy of Ordallia, sign peace terms with Ivalice, and demand a place in the Leadership Games. If he wins, Ivalice is united under Ordallia without a drop of blood. If he loses, Ordallia keeps a foothold in the new court.

Lucius rides west on Nocturne. Aveline follows her father’s compass. In the neutral ruins of the old capital, five houses are gathering to choose a king, and both roads lead to the same city.
